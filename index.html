Progress

Each game remembers every item she meets. Ones she gets wrong come back more often until she gets them right a few times in a row; ones she knows come back every few days so she keeps them.

Her name

Write it with capitals the way it should look. Honey Keys teaches her to type it; Reading Nest asks her to find it among other names.

Her name in your voice

Computer voices often get names wrong. Record her name once and Pip plays your recording whenever it says it. Say just the name, close to the microphone.

Pip's voice

Voices with "Natural" in the name are the clearest. They need an internet connection; without one the app uses the best voice on the laptop.
