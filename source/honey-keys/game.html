Help

Sound

Voices with "Natural" in the name are the clearest. They need an internet connection; without one the game uses the best voice on the laptop.

Difficulty (adjusts by itself)

Level 1 lights up every key. Level 2 lights a key only after a few seconds. Level 3 hides the faint letters too, so she types from memory, and the letter hunt uses small letters. 2 or fewer mistakes and hints in a round moves up; 8 or more moves down.
