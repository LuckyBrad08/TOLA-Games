Grown-ups corner

She moves her animal with the arrow keys. Each round has 5 puzzles and ends with a sticker. If she stops for a while or keeps bumping into things, the arrow she needs lights up yellow and Pip says which one to press.

Progress

The game remembers each item. Ones she gets wrong come back more often until she gets them right a few times in a row; ones she knows come back every few days so she keeps them.

Puzzles

Sound

Voices with "Natural" in the name are the clearest. They need an internet connection; without one the game uses the best voice on the laptop.

Difficulty (adjusts by itself)

Level 1 is a 5×5 field with almost no trees. Level 3 is 7×7 with more trees and longer paths. A round with 1 hint or fewer moves up a level; 5 or more hints moves down.
