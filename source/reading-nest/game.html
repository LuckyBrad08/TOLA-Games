Progress

The game remembers each sound and word. Ones she gets wrong come back more often until she gets them right a few times in a row; ones she knows come back every few days so she keeps them.

Games on the home screen

Sound

Voices with "Natural" in the name are the clearest. They need an internet connection; without one the game uses the best voice on the laptop.

Difficulty (adjusts by itself)

Each game has 3 levels. 80% or more right first time moves up; 40% or less moves down.
